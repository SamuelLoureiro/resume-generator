# Valuing Academic Experiences, Projects, and Extracurricular Activities

## 1. Academic Experience Is Real Experience
One of the greatest insecurities of university students is the myth that only "formal employment contracts" count as experience. For technology and internship recruiters, well-structured academic experiences carry equal or even greater weight than jobs in unrelated fields.

---

## 2. How to Position Each Type of University Experience

### Undergraduate Research (IC / Research Assistantship)
- **How the market sees it:** Evidence of methodological rigor, discipline, analytical ability, and deep intellectual autonomy.
- **What to emphasize:**
  - Processing and analysis of complex datasets or development of novel algorithms.
  - Reading and practical implementation of international scientific papers.
  - Technical writing and submission of abstracts or papers to symposiums and conferences.
  - Scholarship obtained on academic merit (e.g., CNPq, FAPESP, NSF, or equivalent).

### Junior Enterprise (Student Consulting Firm)
- **How the market sees it:** Practical exposure to corporate routine, engagement with real clients, teamwork under agile methodologies, and delivery deadlines.
- **What to emphasize:**
  - Technologies applied in commercial projects delivered to clients.
  - Agile practices (Scrum, Kanban, sprint ceremonies).
  - Client satisfaction outcomes or internal process improvements.

### University Teaching Assistantship
- **How the market sees it:** Consolidated mastery of the course content, teaching ability, patience, and peer leadership skills.
- **What to emphasize:**
  - Subject taught (e.g., *Algorithms and Data Structures*, *Calculus*, *Databases*).
  - Support to classes of dozens of students with office hours and exercise creation.
  - Grading or assistance evaluating practical programming assignments.

### Programming Marathons and Hackathons
- **How the market sees it:** Agility under pressure, sharp logical reasoning, team collaboration under tight deadlines, and focus on delivering working solutions.
- **What to emphasize:**
  - Stage reached (e.g., *Qualified for the Regional Phase of the ICPC Programming Marathon*).
  - Solution developed in a marathon or hackathon within 24/48 hours.

## 3. The Power of Personal Projects and Portfolio
In cutting-edge areas such as Generative AI and RAG, most traditional universities do not yet have complete dedicated courses in their regular curriculum.

Therefore, **personal projects on GitHub demonstrate immediate proactivity and technical leadership**:

- **The Champion Project Rule:** 1 complete project with clean code, a detailed README, explained architecture, and a demo link is worth more than 10 empty repositories with tutorial clones.
- **Indispensable elements in a project README:**
  1. What the project does and what problem it solves.
  2. Solution architecture and flow diagram.
  3. Technologies and libraries used.
  4. Installation and local reproduction instructions (`git clone`, `pip install`).
  5. Evaluation metrics or interface screenshots.
