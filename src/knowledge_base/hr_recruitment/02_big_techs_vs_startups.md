# Recruitment Differences: Big Techs vs. Startups and Scale-ups

## 1. Two Different Evaluation Profiles
The selection process and the valued candidate profile vary significantly depending on the size and stage of the hiring company. Adapting the tone and emphasis of information in the resume dramatically increases the chances of advancing.

---

## 2. Big Techs and Large Multinationals (Google, Microsoft, Mercado Livre, Nubank, etc.)

### What They Look For
- **Theoretical Foundations and Algorithms:** Ability to solve complex problems with good asymptotic complexity (Big-O).
- **Scale and Robustness:** Interest in how systems serve millions of users and process terabytes of data.
- **Process Discipline:** Value candidates accustomed to software engineering best practices (automated tests, code review, technical documentation, CI/CD).
- **Cultural Fit and Corporate Values:** Collaborative work, respectful communication, diversity of ideas, and alignment with the company mission.

### How to Adapt the Resume for Big Techs
- Highlight core computer science courses (Algorithms, Operating Systems, Networking, Databases).
- Emphasize projects focused on clean architecture, unit tests (`pytest`), modularity, and best practices.
- If you participated in competitive programming (ICPC, Codeforces, LeetCode) or rigorous undergraduate research, make it prominent.

---

## 3. Startups and Fast-Growing Scale-ups

### What They Look For
- **Execution Speed (Pragmatism):** Ability to turn an idea into a functional MVP quickly.
- **End-to-End Developer:** Interns who can connect the database, build the backend, integrate an API, and deploy an interactive interface (Gradio, Streamlit, or React).
- **Familiarity with the Modern Ecosystem:** Use of current productivity and AI tools (LlamaIndex, LangChain, FastAPI, Docker, ChromaDB, Hugging Face).
- **Adaptability:** Willingness to handle ambiguity, changing requirements, and learning new tools on the same day.

### How to Adapt the Resume for Startups
- Highlight links to GitHub repositories and working live demos.
- Show direct practical impact: *"Built a functional prototype in 2 weeks using FastAPI and Gradio"*.
- Spotlight personal projects, hackathons, and creative solutions to real-world problems.

---

## 4. Comparative Matrix for RAG Adaptation

| Criterion | Big Tech / Corporation | Startup / Scale-up |
|---|---|---|
| **Main focus** | Algorithms, data, processes, and fundamentals | Practical solutions, speed, and modern technologies |
| **Objective highlight** | Long-term career development in large-scale engineering | Fast value delivery and hands-on solution building |
| **Projects section** | Focus on architecture, testing, and performance | Focus on functional deliverables, deploys, and modern stack |
| **Desired profile** | Analytical, methodical, and collaborative | Proactive, agile, self-directed, and versatile |
