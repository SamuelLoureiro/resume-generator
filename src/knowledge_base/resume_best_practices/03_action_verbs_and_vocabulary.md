# Action Verb Catalog and High-Impact Vocabulary

## 1. The Importance of Action Verbs
Action verbs position the candidate as an active agent of change, eliminating the passive tone of a "mere participant." In internship screenings, the use of precise technical vocabulary conveys confidence, tool mastery, and professionalism.

---

## 2. Verb Categories by Domain

### Software Development, Architecture, and Engineering
- **Build / Create:** Developed, Implemented, Built, Architected, Structured, Designed, Coded, Engineered.
- **Integration & APIs:** Integrated, Connected, Consumed, Exposed, Standardized, Decoupled, Modularized.
- **Deploy & Infrastructure:** Containerized, Provisioned, Configured, Automated, Orchestrated, Packaged.

### Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Data Engineering
- **Modeling & Training:** Trained, Fine-tuned, Evaluated, Validated, Calibrated, Converged.
- **Pipelines & ETL:** Extracted, Processed, Normalized, Ingested, Transformed, Cleaned, Structured.
- **Retrieval and NLP:** Indexed, Vectorized, Retrieved, Embedded, Chunked, Ranked.
- **Analysis & Metrics:** Measured, Analyzed, Quantified, Benchmarked, Compared, Identified.

### Optimization, Problem Solving, and Performance
- **Technical Improvement:** Optimized, Accelerated, Reduced, Refactored, Debugged, Resolved, Enhanced, Minimized.
- **Quality & Reliability:** Tested, Assured, Validated, Covered, Documented, Hardened.

### Student Leadership, Initiative, and Collaboration
- **Management & Communication:** Led, Coordinated, Articulated, Presented, Facilitated, Mentored, Guided.
- **Methodologies:** Applied Scrum/Kanban, Iterated, Planned, Aligned requirements.

---

## 3. Blacklist: Weak Words and Expressions to Avoid

| Weak Expression | Why Avoid It | High-Impact Substitute |
|---|---|---|
| "Was responsible for..." | Passive; reads like a job contract duty list. | "Managed", "Coordinated", "Executed". |
| "Helped the team to..." | Diminishes your actual contribution. | "Collaborated on developing...", "Implemented the module for...". |
| "Worked with Python..." | Vague; doesn't say what was built or what the result was. | "Developed Python scripts to automate...". |
| "Had exposure to..." | Implies superficial knowledge and insecurity. | "Applied concepts of...", "Implemented routines using...". |
| "Did daily tasks..." | Generic; zero added value. | "Executed operational workflows...", "Optimized the flow of...". |
| "I learn quickly" | Empty cliché in a skills section. | Show adoption of new stacks in concrete projects. |

---

## 4. Consistency and Lexical Variation
- Avoid repeating the same verb at the start of consecutive bullets (e.g., do not start 3 lines in a row with "Developed").
- Alternate between development, optimization, testing, and collaboration to showcase a well-rounded profile.
- Keep technical terms in their standard English form (e.g., *Machine Learning*, *Pipeline*, *Feature Store*, *Framework*).
