# Common Mistakes and Fatal Red Flags in Internship Resumes

## 1. The 6-Second Filter
Research consistently shows that human recruiters perform an initial visual scan in 6 to 10 seconds. Small oversights or out-of-standard elements are enough to disqualify a candidate at this preliminary screening.

---

## 2. The Main Red Flags That Eliminate Candidates

### 1. Grammatical and Typing Errors
- **Severity:** High (the #1 reason for immediate rejection).
- **Impact:** Signals lack of attention to detail, carelessness in proofreading, and poor written communication skills.
- **Special attention:** Technology names matter — misspelling "Phyton", "Javacript", "Github", "Fast Api" instead of *Python*, *JavaScript*, *GitHub*, *FastAPI* leaves a very poor technical impression.

### 2. Generic "One-Size-Fits-All" Resume
- **Severity:** High.
- **Impact:** Sending the same generic resume to a Data Engineering position and a Frontend Web Development position. The recruiter notices in seconds that the candidate did not read the job description.
- **Solution:** The professional objective, the ordering of technologies, and the featured projects must be adjusted for each specific position.

### 3. Lack of Context or Passive Bullets
- **Severity:** Medium-High.
- **Impact:** Bullets such as *"Helped maintain the database"* or *"Attended weekly meetings"* do not communicate what was learned, which technology was used, or what result was achieved.

### 4. Broken or Inaccessible Links
- **Severity:** Medium-High.
- **Impact:** The recruiter clicks on the GitHub or Portfolio link and finds a 404 error, an empty repository with only a README and no code, or an outdated LinkedIn profile.
- **Rule:** If you include a link to a project, ensure the repository has organized code, documentation, and clear execution instructions.

### 5. Inclusion of Unnecessary Personal Information
- **Severity:** Medium.
- **Impact:** Visually clutters the resume and wastes precious space on a single page.
- **Never include:**
  - Photo (unless explicitly required by the position — extremely rare in tech).
  - National ID numbers (SSN, passport number, driver's license).
  - Full home address with street and apartment number (City/State is sufficient).
  - Salary expectations (a topic for the interview stage).
  - Parents' names or unsolicited personal references.

### 6. Skill Hallucination (Overclaiming)
- **Severity:** Critical.
- **Impact:** Listing a technology the student has only "heard about" or watched a 15-minute video on. If called for a technical interview and unable to explain the basics, the candidate is disqualified and permanently loses credibility with the company.
- **RAG Rule:** The resume generator must use **only** competencies and data present in the candidate's real profile (`candidate_profile.yaml`).

---

## 3. Final Validation Checklist Before Submission
- [ ] The resume fits exactly on 1 PDF page without overflow.
- [ ] There are no spelling errors in technical terms, library names, or grammar.
- [ ] All links (LinkedIn, GitHub, projects) work and open to the correct pages.
- [ ] The professional objective specifically mentions the opportunity and the target area.
- [ ] The top 3 skills required by the job are evidenced in practice through projects or experiences.
